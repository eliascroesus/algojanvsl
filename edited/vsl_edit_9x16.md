# vsl_edit_9x16.mp4

The same edit as [`vsl_edit.mp4`](vsl_edit.md) (same cuts, timing, text, cards, music and sound), laid out for a
**1080×1920 (9:16)** frame for Reels, TikTok, Shorts and story ads.

Rebuild: run the same commands as the 16:9 version with `VSL_FORMAT=9x16` set, e.g.
`VSL_FORMAT=9x16 python tools/edit_vsl.py RAW WORK video 0 154 out.mp4`. The audio is the same file as the 16:9 version.

## What changes for the tall frame

- **Framing:** a 9:16 crop of the 16:9 footage centred on Jan. The wide shots fill the height of the frame, and the
  close shots and punch-ins push in further. The source is 1080p, so the picture is upscaled about 1.8× (with light
  sharpening). It's slightly softer than the 16:9 version; this is the most a 1080p recording allows in portrait.
- **Text beside his head** becomes white over gold, centred on his shirt below the mic.
- **The rows of three cards** stack vertically, with connectors and strike-throughs running down the stack.
- **Photos** pop up over his chest instead of beside him.
- **The certificate push** centres on the FTMO plaque.
- **Pages** have the same text, re-spaced for the tall frame (long lines shrink to fit).
- **The montage** is re-laid to fill the tall frame.
- **The offer:** Jan stays at the top, and the "Here's what *you get*" panel slides up from the bottom and builds line by line.
- **Captions** sit lower, above the bottom safe area; long phrases shrink to fit the width.

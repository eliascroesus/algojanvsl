# Other creator: raw VSL

The raw, unedited VSL of **another creator** (not Algo Jan). It will be edited in the same style as Jan's VSL
(AB Nasdaq structure, Ricky's design).

**Upload here (up to 2 GB per file):**
[Other creator raw VSL release](https://github.com/eliascroesus/algojanvsl/releases/new?tag=other-creator-raw-vsl&title=Other%20creator%20raw%20VSL)

1. Open the link. If GitHub asks about the tag, pick **Create new tag: other-creator-raw-vsl on publish**.
2. Drag the video into the box at the bottom ("Attach binaries by dropping them here").
3. Wait until the upload bar has finished, then click **Publish release**.

In the release description, it helps to add the creator's name or brand, what they sell, and any results, photos or
reference videos you want used.

To add more files later (results, photos of the creator, a song), open the release under **Releases**, click the
pencil (Edit), attach them and click **Update release**.
